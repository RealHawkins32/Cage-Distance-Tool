Method and limitations

All scores run from 0 (least distance) to 10 (greatest distance). Scores are reproducible screening heuristics, not official CAGE scores or forecasts. Cultural indicators are historical Hofstede-style country-index estimates, not live measurements. Administrative scores use a curated, non-exhaustive list of selected trade agreements; sanctions are not automatically checked. Geographic distance uses capital-city great-circle distance, not actual shipping routes. Logistics Performance Index values are from 2023. Economic data uses the latest World Bank GDP-per-capita observations that the browser can retrieve.

If current World Bank income data cannot be retrieved, the tool withholds the economic score and overall recommendation rather than inventing numbers. Verify trade agreements, sanctions, tariffs, cultural-index licensing and methodology, and firm-specific feasibility before relying on this assessment.

Scoring rules: cultural = mean absolute difference across four 0–100 cultural indicators ÷ 10; administrative = 2 when a qualifying trade arrangement is documented; otherwise unscored pending verification (absence from this list does not prove no agreement exists); geographic = 75% of min(10, capital distance in km ÷ 1,500) + 25% of min(10, |LPI difference| × 5), reduced by 1 for a shared land border, bounded 0–10; economic = 10 × |GDP per capita A − B| ÷ max(A,B). Overall = Σ(score × weight/100). These coefficients are explicit analyst choices and can be revised; they are not calibrated predictions.

Primary sources: The Culture Factor country comparison · WTO RTA database · OFAC sanctions · CEPII GeoDist · World Bank LPI · World Bank GDP per capita.
