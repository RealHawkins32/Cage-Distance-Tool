Method and limitations

Each dimension receives a judgment-based rating from 0 to 10, where 0 represents minimal distance and 10 represents extreme distance. Industry-specific weights must total exactly 100%.

The weighted average is a screening heuristic, not a validated prediction of expansion success. Ratings are provisional and should be checked against current country data before investment decisions.

The tool does not evaluate firm capabilities, product-specific regulations, tariffs, or actual consumer demand.
