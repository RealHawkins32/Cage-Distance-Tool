CAGE Distance Framework

Evidence-backed, illustrative country-pair assessment. Distance scores are decision aids, not forecasts.

Current data coverage: United States ↔ Japan only. Other pairs are deliberately not simulated.

Method and limitations

Each dimension receives a judgment-based 0–10 distance rating (0 = low friction; 10 = high friction). Weights reflect industry exposure, sum to 100%, and are editable. The weighted average is a screening heuristic, not a validated prediction of expansion success. Ratings are provisional and should be independently checked before investment decisions.

Scores use cited country-level reference sources. They do not account for firm capabilities, current tariffs, product regulations, or a specific distribution channel. Dates and quantitative values should be checked against current releases.
